FAQ

Fair questions

Will my tenants know they’re talking to an AI?

Yes. RentShepherd introduces itself as your property’s assistant and never pretends to be you. Tenants can ask for you directly at any time, and you can jump into any thread whenever you want.

What happens in a genuine emergency at 2am?

The triage engine treats anything involving water, gas, electrical hazard, no heat, or security as urgent. The tenant gets immediate safety guidance, you get an alert with the full context, and a vendor dispatch draft is waiting for your one-tap approval. For life-threatening situations, tenants are always told to contact local emergency services first.

Can RentShepherd do anything without my approval?

Only what you’ve pre-approved — routine replies, scheduled rent reminders, logging requests. Anything new that commits your money or your word — sending a vendor, making a renewal offer, promising a repair date — waits for your one-tap approval. You can widen or tighten those boundaries per property.

Does RentShepherd collect rent or hold my money?

You choose. Turn on automatic payments and tenants pay through the renter portal — autopay sends rent straight to your bank account through a licensed payment processor, and RentShepherd never holds your funds. Prefer your current setup? It works alongside however your tenants already pay you, tracking who has paid and flagging late payments either way.

Does it work outside the United States?

RentShepherd is being built for landlords worldwide, and the early access list is open to landlords anywhere. Tenant messaging, the maintenance inbox, rent tracking, renewals, and owner reports are designed to work anywhere; phone-call answering will depend on local number availability in your country. Planned billing is in USD. Wherever you are, our privacy policy sets out exactly what we collect when you join the list.

I run a property management company — can RentShepherd handle a whole portfolio?

Yes. There is no unit cap: RentShepherd can sit on the front line of every building you manage — as many units, houses, and buildings as you have. Tenant conversations are answered and triaged per property, your team gets the approvals, replies go out under your company’s name, and your owners get clean per-building reports. Join the list below and choose “Property management company” — we’ll set up your portfolio together.

How do I get access?

RentShepherd is in early access and not yet open to the public — there is no sign-up, no checkout and no account to create. Join the early access list with your name, your email, and roughly how many units you look after, and we will email you when access opens for you, along with what it costs and what your options are. Joining the list is free and commits you to nothing.
