The features Texas landlords lean on most

From the first tenant text to the monthly report — here's what RentShepherd takes off your plate in Texas.

Triage that knows a burst pipe from a dripping tap

An air-conditioning failure in the Texas summer heat is treated as the emergency it is. Genuine emergencies escalate to you immediately, with safety guidance already sent to the tenant. Everything else is logged, prioritized, and scheduled for a reasonable hour.

Work orders with photos, start to finish

Instead of a vague voicemail, you get a structured work order: photos, video, unit, and description, submitted through the renter portal and tracked to resolution. Your Texas tenants see progress; you see everything.

Rent that collects itself

Autopay through the renter portal sends rent straight to your account on the first — no chasing, no awkward texts. Prefer your current setup? RentShepherd still tracks and flags every Texas payment either way.

Lease renewals, never last-minute

90-, 60-, and 30-day renewal checkpoints with drafted tenant outreach, so notice periods never sneak past you — and good Texas tenants get a renewal offer before they start browsing listings.

Tenant calls and texts, covered around the clock

One number for every tenant in your Texas portfolio. Texts get instant, accurate replies; calls are answered, triaged, and transcribed. Nothing rings through to you at midnight unless it truly should.
